## What belongs in this list

An entry should meet **all** of the following:

1. **Board-game-design focus.** It is made for, or clearly useful to, people
   *designing* board games (prototyping, rules, art, production, publishing,
   playtesting, community). General-purpose tools need a concrete board-game
   use case explained in the PR.
2. **Identifiable and maintained.** A named operator or maintainer, a working
   site, and signs of activity in the last 12 months.
3. **Track record.** In use for a few months at minimum, ideally with users
   from the board game community.
4. **Honest description.** One line, factual, no marketing language. Mention
   cost (free / freemium / paid) and whether an account is required.
5. **Disclose your connection.** If you made or maintain the resource, say so
   in the PR. It doesn't disqualify it, but it gets a closer look.
6. **One entry per PR**, placed in the best-fitting category, alphabetical
   within the category.

Entries may be declined or removed if they stop meeting these criteria.
